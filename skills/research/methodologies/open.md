# Open (default)

## Settings

- Rumors: labeled
- Citation style: footnotes
- Translations: offer

## Sources

Every type is admissible in the text, including forums, imageboards and social media.
Claims resting only on D or E sources name their source in the sentence.

## Confirmation

The standard scale in SKILL.md, unchanged.

## In the text

The standard wording rules in SKILL.md: credibility 3 reads as a rumor with its origin,
4-5 only if it matters, 6 left out.

## Extra checks

None.
