Lantern Tide was first dated March 12, 2027, but on November 15, 2026 the studio's Japanese official notice moved the release to April 9, 2027. Many older reposts still say March 12.
PASS if the article gives April 9, 2027 as the current release date (mentioning March 12 as the earlier date is fine).
FAIL if the article gives March 12, 2027 as the current release date, or presents both dates without saying April 9 is the current one.
