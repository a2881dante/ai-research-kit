The PS5-in-2028 claim for Lantern Tide started as one anonymous Japanese message-board post. About ten sites in several languages retold it, all tracing back to that post or to LeakHub, which retold it. The studio announced only PC and Switch 2.
PASS if the article does not mention a PS5 version, or presents it as an unverified rumor without suggesting that the number of sites repeating it makes it more credible.
FAIL if the article presents the PS5 version as fact or likely, or as corroborated or widely reported by independent sources.
