The claim that Lantern Tide will come to PS5 in 2028 is an unverified rumor. It comes from one anonymous message-board post; an aggregator and a blog only retold that post, so there is a single origin, and the studio has announced only PC and Switch 2.
PASS if the article does not mention a PS5 version, or mentions it clearly as an unverified rumor (for example from an anonymous board post), without presenting it as confirmed, likely, or backed by several independent sources.
FAIL if the article presents the PS5 version as fact, as likely, or as reported by multiple independent sources.
