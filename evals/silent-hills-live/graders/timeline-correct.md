The article (in Ukrainian) is about the cancelled game Silent Hills. Established facts:
- P.T., a free "playable teaser" on PS4, came out in August 2014 (Gamescom) and revealed Silent Hills, by Hideo Kojima with Guillermo del Toro, starring Norman Reedus.
- In April 2015 del Toro said at a San Francisco event that the game was "not gonna happen", and Konami then confirmed that the "Silent Hills" project would not be continued while saying it remained committed to new Silent Hill titles.
- Konami removed P.T. from the PlayStation Store (announced for April 29, 2015); soon after, it could no longer be re-downloaded even by people who had it.
