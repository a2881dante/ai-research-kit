Konami never officially explained why it cancelled Silent Hills: its statement only said the project would not be continued. The most widely cited explanation, the falling-out between Hideo Kojima and Konami in 2015 (Kojima's name removed from Metal Gear Solid V marketing in March 2015, his departure from Konami later that year), and others such as Konami's shift towards mobile games, are interpretations by press, commentators and the people involved, not reasons Konami confirmed.
