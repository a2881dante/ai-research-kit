Later rumors about Silent Hills:
- In 2020, insiders claimed Sony was reviving Silent Hill / Silent Hills, possibly with Kojima. Konami said the reports were "not true".
- In 2021-2022, fans claimed that the indie game Abandoned (Blue Box Game Studios, director Hasan Kahraman) was secretly Kojima's Silent Hill. Blue Box denied any relation with Konami or Kojima, and Kojima called the rumors "quite a nuisance".
