# Lantern Tide launches March 12, 2027

Kumo Forge has dated Lantern Tide: the narrative exploration game arrives on March 12, 2027 on PC and Nintendo Switch 2, priced at $24.99. Director Aiko Mura describes it as a story about a city that remembers. Pre-orders are open now.
