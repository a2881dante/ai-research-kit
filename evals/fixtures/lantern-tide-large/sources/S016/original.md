# Lantern Fall gets a May 2027 date on PS5

Brightwick Studios' action game Lantern Fall launches on May 20, 2027 on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S for $39.99. Director Paul Hendry promises a 30-hour campaign. Lantern Fall is a sequel to Ember Fall.
